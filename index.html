How it works

1

Download & Open

Drag to Applications, right-click → Open, complete the 60-second setup

2

Hold & Speak

Hold your chosen key and talk naturally. The mic records while held.

3

Release & Paste

Release the key. Your words appear at your cursor in under a second.

Your voice stays on your Mac.
