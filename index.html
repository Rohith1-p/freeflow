02 / HOW IT WORKS

Three steps.
Under a minute.

macOS asks for two permissions. The model downloads once. Then it's yours.

STEP 01

Drag & open

Drag Freeflow to Applications and launch it. Signed and notarized.

STEP 02

Grant & download

A one-minute wizard handles permissions and the ~465 MB model download.

STEP 03

Hold & speak

Hold ⌃ Control, talk naturally, release. Your words land at the cursor.
